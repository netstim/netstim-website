<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-1"><h2>Andreas Horn receives International Brain Stimulation Early Career Award</h2>
<p>We are excited to share that Andreas has been recognized with the 2025 International Brain Stimulation Early Career Award.</p>
<p>He presented a plenary lecture at the Brain Stimulation meeting in Kobe, Japan, February 23-26, 2025.</p>
