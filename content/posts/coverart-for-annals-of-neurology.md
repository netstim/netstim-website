<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><p>We’re excited that results from <a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ana.25734">the connectomic DBS study spearheaded by Friederike Irmen </a>was featured on the coverart of the journal.</p>
<p>Friederike used structural connectivity metrics seeding from DBS electrodes reconstructed in <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org">Lead-DBS</a> to estimate and validate connectivity profiles that were associated with changes in depressive symptoms following deep brain stimulation to the subthalamic nucleus for treatment of Parkinson’s Disease.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ana.25734">full study can be found here</a> or on our <a href="@root@about-us/publications/">publications page</a>.</p>
