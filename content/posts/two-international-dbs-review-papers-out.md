<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><p>We’re happy that our laboratory could play a small role in two larger international review papers about DBS technology.</p>
<p>The first one, organized by the <a href="https://www.wssfn.org/">WSSFN</a> and spearheaded by Joachim Krauss &amp; Andres Lozano <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41582-020-00426-z">reviews the current status and future directions of DBS technology</a>.</p>
</div><div class="column column-1-3"><div class="image-block"><a aria-label="Bildschirmfoto 2021-03-04 um 09.02.22" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41582-020-00426-z"><netstim-image data='{"src":"media/739e60dc2e29-Bildschirmfoto-2021-03-04-um-09.02.22.png","original":"https://www.netstim.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Bildschirmfoto-2021-03-04-um-09.02.22.png","alt":"","width":1282}'/></a></div></div></div>

<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-3"><div class="image-block"><a aria-label="Bildschirmfoto 2021-03-04 um 09.04.41" href="https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fneur.2020.597451/full"><netstim-image data='{"src":"media/fa2b591db727-Bildschirmfoto-2021-03-04-um-09.04.41.png","original":"https://www.netstim.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Bildschirmfoto-2021-03-04-um-09.04.41.png","alt":"","width":1674}'/></a></div></div><div class="column column-2-3"><p>The second one was built <a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fneur.2020.597451/full">on proceedings of the Super-DBS workshop in Beijing, 2018 and is spearheaded by Luming Li, Suzanne Haber and Hesheng Liu</a>.</p>
</div></div>

<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-1"><p>Both review papers may provide a good overview about the status quo and the future of DBS and are central to the aims of our network stimulation laboratory.</p>
</div></div>
