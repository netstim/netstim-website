<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-1"><h2>Bahne Bahners ‘Junior Awardee’, Plenary Lecture &amp; Featured in Podcast @ MDS</h2>
<p>We are excited that Bahne Bahners was recognized as ‘Junior Awardee’ for his abstract ‘Understanding the optimal brain fingerprint to improve DBS outcomes in Parkinson’s disease’ at the 2024 International Meeting of the Movement Disorders Society (MDS).</p>
<p>To celebrate this prize, Bahne will present his work in a plenary talk within the presidential symposium at the MDS 2024 meeting in Philadelphia next week!</p>
