<article class="post-item"><div class="post-image"><a aria-label="2018-Annals_of_Neurology-1" href="@root@wp-content/uploads/2018/11/2018-Annals_of_Neurology-1-1-783x1030.png" title="2018-Annals_of_Neurology-1"><netstim-image data='{"src":"media/a8e9659bf64e-2018-Annals_of_Neurology-1-1-845x321.png","original":"https://www.netstim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/2018-Annals_of_Neurology-1-1-845x321.png","alt":"","width":845}'/></a></div><div class="standard-content"><header aria-label="Post: Lead-DBS on the cover of Annals of Neurology"><h2>Lead-DBS on the cover of Annals of Neurology</h2><span class="post-meta"><time datetime="2018-11-04T18:08:51+00:00">November 4, 2018</time>/<a href="@root@lead-dbs-on-the-cover-of-annals-of-neurology/#respond">0 Comments</a>/in <a href="@root@category/uncategorized/">Uncategorized</a>/by <a href="@root@author/andreashorn/">andreashorn</a></span></header><p>We’re really excited that the current cover of AoN was made with Lead-DBS. It shows pallidal and thalamic electrodes simultaneously implanted in a patient suffering from Tourette’s Syndrom and the picture is part of an electrophysiological Berlin-Hannover study spearheaded by Wolf-Julian Neumann and led by Andrea Kühn. It is the sixth coverart made with Lead-DBS that we know of – <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org/about/publications" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">a gallery can be found here</a>.</p>
