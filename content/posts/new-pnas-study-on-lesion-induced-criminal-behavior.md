<article class="post-item"><div class="post-image"><a aria-label="F1.medium" href="@root@wp-content/uploads/2017/12/F1.medium.gif" title="F1.medium"><netstim-image data='{"src":"media/6f43bad24b1e-F1.medium.gif","original":"https://www.netstim.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/F1.medium.gif","alt":"","width":440}'/></a></div><div class="standard-content"><header aria-label="Post: New PNAS Study on Lesion induced criminal behavior"><h2>New PNAS Study on Lesion induced criminal behavior</h2><span class="post-meta"><time datetime="2017-12-29T14:26:01+00:00">December 29, 2017</time>/<a href="@root@new-pnas-study-on-lesion-induced-criminal-behavior/#respond">0 Comments</a>/in <a href="@root@category/uncategorized/">Uncategorized</a>/by <a href="@root@author/andreashorn/">andreashorn</a></span></header><p>We just published a <a href="http://www.pnas.org/content/early/2017/12/12/1706587115">new study</a> spearheaded by R. Ryan Darby that used the Lesion Network Mapping approach developed by Michael Fox to analyse <strong>lesion induced criminal behavior</strong>.</p>
<p>The study has been featured on <a href="https://www.altmetric.com/details/30629132/news">multiple news outlets</a> such as <a href="http://www.wsfa.com/story/37130014/vanderbilt-doctor-finds-link-between-criminal-behavior-and-brain-trauma">WSFA</a>, <a href="http://www.spiegel.de/gesundheit/diagnose/charles-whitman-verwandeln-hirnverletzungen-menschen-in-moerder-a-1184534.html#ref=rss">Der Spiegel</a>, The Independent and <a href="https://elpais.com/elpais/2017/12/19/ciencia/1513705212_349792.html">El País</a>.</p>
<p>Findings show that lesions leading to criminal behavior are connected to a common brain network. This criminality-associated connectivity pattern was unique compared with lesions causing four other neuropsychiatric syndromes.</p>
