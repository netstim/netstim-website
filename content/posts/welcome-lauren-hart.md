<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><h2>We are pleased to welcome Lauren Hart to the lab</h2>
<p>Lauren has joined us as a first research assistant within the Center for Brain Circuit Therapeutics at Brigham &amp; Women’s Hospital in Boston.</p>
<p>Lauren will help coordinate multiple projects and as a main focus will curate &amp; maintain databases of neuromodulation datasets &amp; analyze involved connectomic profiles. She will also help with Lead-DBS user support, website management and professionalizing Lead-DBS documentation (such as manual &amp; walkthrough videos).</p>
