<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-1"><h2>NeuroImage 2018 Best Paper Award</h2>
</div></div>

<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-3"><div class="image-block"><netstim-image data='{"src":"media/200ea4ea257d-IMG_4316-2.jpeg","original":"https://www.netstim.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/IMG_4316-2-1030x556.jpeg","alt":"","width":1030}'/></div></div><div class="column column-2-3"><p>We’re extremely pleased that one of our papers was awarded with the Elsevier NeuroImage Best Paper Award 2018 at the annual Organization for Human Brain Mapping (OHBM) meeting 2019 in Rome.</p>
<p><a href="https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/S1053811918318664">The paper</a>, spearheaded by Siobhan Ewert, established the main subcortical atlas now used within our deep brain stimulation reconstruction software <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org">Lead-DBS</a>. It used structural MRI, diffusion-weighted imaging based tractography as well as histology to create a precise patchwork atlas within ICBM 2009b NLIN Asym (“MNI”) space that so far has been applied <a href="https://scholar.google.de/scholar?oi=bibs&amp;hl=en&amp;cites=3844964309813058582">in various clinical and translational papers</a>.</p>
</div></div>

<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><p>The histology dataset used to create the atlas was graciously supplied by Mallar M. Chakravarty and D. Louis Collins from the MNI, which are both co-authors on the paper, as well.</p>
<p><a href="https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/S1053811918318664">The paper</a> was selected from over 3000 manuscripts submitted to NeuroImage within the course of 2018. It was cited cited &gt;60 times within 18 months and has thoroughly empowered the <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org">Lead-DBS software package</a>.</p>
