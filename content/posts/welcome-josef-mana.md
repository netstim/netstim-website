<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><h2>We are pleased to welcome Josef Mana to the lab</h2>
<p>Josef is enrolled as a PhD student at Charles University in Prague and is visiting us for a lab rotation during his PhD. He is pursuing his PhD in the laboratory of Robert Jech about the topic “Cognitive predictors of conversion to dementia in Parkinson’s disease patients treated with Deep Brain Stimulation”.</p>
<p>With us, Josef will collaborate on multiple projects and as a main focus will study network effects of subthalamic nucleus deep brain stimulation on cognition. We are excited to welcome Josef in Berlin &amp; look forward to the beginning of a series of fruitful collaborations.</p>
