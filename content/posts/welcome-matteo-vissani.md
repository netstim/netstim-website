<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><h2>We are pleased to welcome Matteo Vissani to the lab</h2>
<p>Matteo is enrolled as a PhD student at Scuola Superiore Sant’Anna in Pisa and is visiting us for a lab rotation during his PhD.</p>
<p>He has published exciting work on treating persons with <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/334859140_Spatio-temporal_structure_of_single_neuron_subthalamic_activity_identifies_DBS_target_for_anesthetized_Tourette_Syndrome_patients?_sg=T0VA4arfrKgyICZazpg3eigZuHbTubeVt5eoOnwg1Y7QWETubk2GTFBfUsSnmqde3GcbNkL7e1rODpTypBjo1rTwYjrfd0398jLS7dmv.A8leB6_sQOCbIFNS_7_IQdKrM1SRU_7k_F5ZDwgBxq33g3w8stwYF1OR1Vr5wm5egK3Z9VxA-Tx6qAhTvYQLpg">Tourette’s Disease with deep brain stimulation to the subthalamic nucleus</a> and recently a review about <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/344559578_Deep_brain_stimulation_a_review_of_the_open_neural_engineering_challenges?_sg=T0VA4arfrKgyICZazpg3eigZuHbTubeVt5eoOnwg1Y7QWETubk2GTFBfUsSnmqde3GcbNkL7e1rODpTypBjo1rTwYjrfd0398jLS7dmv.A8leB6_sQOCbIFNS_7_IQdKrM1SRU_7k_F5ZDwgBxq33g3w8stwYF1OR1Vr5wm5egK3Z9VxA-Tx6qAhTvYQLpg">open challenges in deep brain stimulation</a>.</p>
<p>With us, Matteo will collaborate on multiple projects and as a main focus will implement a meta-search engine for querying subcortical an cortical atlases of the brain. We are excited to welcome Matteo in Berlin &amp; look forward to the beginning of a series of fruitful collaborations.</p>
