<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><p>We’re excited that results from <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338625914_Deep_Brain_Stimulation_Imaging_on_a_group_level">the paper about our novel toolbox, <em>Lead Group</em>, spearheaded by Svenja Treu</a> was featured on the coverart of the NeuroImage.</p>
<p>Svenja described methods &amp; a novel open source toolbox to shift deep brain stimulation analyses onto a group level in a standardized and reproducible way.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338625914_Deep_Brain_Stimulation_Imaging_on_a_group_level">full study can be found here</a> or on our <a href="http://www.netstim.berlin/about-us/publications/">publications page</a>.</p>
