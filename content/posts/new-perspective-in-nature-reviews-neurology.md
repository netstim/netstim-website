<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><h2><b>New Perspective in </b></h2>
<h3><b>Nature Reviews Neurology</b></h3>
<p>We are excited to share that our latest Perspective article, <b>“<a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41582-025-01131-5">From adaptive deep brain stimulation to adaptive circuit targeting</a>”</b> has just been published in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41582-025-01131-5"><i>Nature Reviews Neurology</i></a>.</p>
<p>In this piece, Andreas Horn (Netstim Cologne) and Wolf-Julian Neumann (Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin) propose a unified framework that integrates two major recent advances in the field of deep brain stimulation (DBS):</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><b>Adaptive DBS</b> – stimulation that responds in real time to brain signals and symptom states.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><b>Connectomic DBS</b> – stimulation guided by brain network maps that identify circuits linked to symptom improvement.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>We propose the concept of <b>adaptive circuit targeting (ACT)</b>, which combines these approaches by decoding symptom severity from neural signals and adaptively steering stimulation toward the most relevant brain circuits.</p>
<p>This framework highlights future opportunities for more precise, individualized, and effective neuromodulation therapies.</p>
<p>📖 Read the full article here: <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41582-025-01131-5">Nature Reviews Neurology</a></p>
