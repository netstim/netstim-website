<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3"><h2>Barbara Hollunder receives Thiemann Fellowship at the German Neurology Society (DGN) meeting</h2>
<p>We are excited to share that Barbara has been recognized with the 2025 Thiemann Fellowship.</p>
<p>She will use the funds for a postdoctoral stay under the supervision of Dr. Michael D Fox at the Center for Brain Circuit Therapeutics at Harvard Medical School / Mass General Brigham.</p>
<p>We are proud that following Andreas, Lukas and Bahne, Barbara is the fourth trainee with a stipend of the foundation that either comes out of or went into the Network Stimulation Laboratory.</p>
