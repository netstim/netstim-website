<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-1"><h2>Coverart for BRAIN</h2>
</div></div>

<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-3"><div class="image-block"><netstim-image data='{"src":"media/1248dda95d07-IMG_5825.png","original":"https://www.netstim.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/IMG_5825-232x300.png","alt":"","width":232}'/></div></div><div class="column column-2-3"><p>We’re excited that results from <a href="https://academic.oup.com/brain/article-abstract/142/10/3086/5543457?redirectedFrom=fulltext">the connectomic DBS study spearheaded by Bassam Al-Fatly</a> was featured on the coverart of the journal.</p>
<p>Bassam used structural and functional connectivity metrics seeding from electrodes reconstructed in Lead-DBS to estimate and validate connectivity profiles that were associated with beneficial treatment effects of deep brain stimulation to the ventral intermediate nucleus for treatment of Essential Tremor Syndrome.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://academic.oup.com/brain/article-abstract/142/10/3086/5543457?redirectedFrom=fulltext">full study can be found here</a> or on our <a href="@root@about-us/publications/">publications page</a>.</p>
