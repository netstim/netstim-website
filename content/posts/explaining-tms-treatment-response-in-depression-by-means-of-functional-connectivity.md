Specifically, strength of anticorrelations between DLPFC and subgenual were predictive of beneficial outcome. This relationship had been hypothesized by <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Michael_Fox11">Michael D Fox</a> and colleagues in 2012 (<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/225094403_Efficacy_of_Transcranial_Magnetic_Stimulation_Targets_for_Depression_Is_Related_to_Intrinsic_Functional_Connectivity_with_the_Subgenual_Cingulate">Efficacy of Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation Targets for De…</a>) and was now prospectively validated in a sample of 25 patients treated at BIDMC with TMS.
Moreover, to exclude sham effects, a cohort of 16 patients treated by <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Stephan_Taylor">Stephan F Taylor</a> in Michigan was retrospectively analyzed and showed the same relationship.
