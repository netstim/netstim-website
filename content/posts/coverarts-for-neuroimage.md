<div class="columns"><div class="column column-1-1"><p>We’re excited that after results from <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338625914_Deep_Brain_Stimulation_Imaging_on_a_group_level">the paper about our novel toolbox, <em>Lead Group</em>, spearheaded by Svenja Treu</a> was featured on the coverart of the NeuroImage, two additional papers in 2020 were featured, as well.</p>
<p>The first is from a <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S105381192030793X">comparison study between using normative and patient-specific connectomes to predict clinical outcomes in Parkinson’s Disease, spearheaded by Qiang Wang</a>. For this effort, the lab collaborated with the London, Mt. Sinai and Mainz DBS centers. The second is based on a <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053811920306662">review paper about opportunities in connectomic DBS written by Andreas Horn and Michael Fox</a>.</p>
<p>All studies &amp; cover pages can be found on our <a href="@root@about-us/publications/">publications page</a>.</p>
