Andreas Horn is Director of the Institute for Network Stimulation at University Hospital Cologne and Schilling Foundation Professor of Computational Neurology at the University of Cologne. He studies basal ganglia and cortical networks using the human connectome and lesion network mapping to find symptom-specific deep brain stimulation (DBS) targets. He created [Lead-DBS](@root@research/lead-dbs/), an open-source toolbox for DBS imaging, developed with groups from over 20 institutions. He edited [*Connectomic Deep Brain Stimulation*](@root@book/) (2021) and hosts the [Stimulating Brains](https://www.stimulatingbrains.org) podcast.

Andreas earned his Dr. med. at the University of Freiburg (2012) and an MD/PhD at Charité Berlin (2016), where he founded Netstim in 2019. From 2021 to 2025, he was Associate Professor of Neurology at Harvard Medical School and directed DBS research at Brigham and Women’s Hospital before moving Netstim to Cologne. He won the German Research Foundation (DFG) Heinz Maier-Leibnitz Prize in 2022 and the Falling Walls Global Call in life sciences in 2024. In 2025, he received the International Brain Stimulation Early Career Award.
