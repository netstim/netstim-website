Published by Academic Press in September 2021, *Connectomic Deep Brain Stimulation* is 600 pages long and includes color figures. Following a foreword by Andrea Kühn and Michael Fox, 26 chapters cover electrode localization, DBS modeling, connectomics, and clinical applications in movement disorders, depression, and OCD.

While most of the book focuses on neuroimaging methods, additional chapters cover electrophysiology, histological and postmortem data, and dynamical models.

The book can be ordered [from the publisher](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/connectomic-deep-brain-stimulation/horn/978-0-12-821861-7) or [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Connectomic-Deep-Brain-Stimulation-Andreas/dp/0128218614/), or read online on [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/9780128218617/connectomic-deep-brain-stimulation).
