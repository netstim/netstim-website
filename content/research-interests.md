## Research Interests

<p>Our two main lines of research are <a href="http://andreas-horn.de/?page_id=151">Deep Brain Stimulation</a> and <a href="http://andreas-horn.de/?page_id=110">Connectomics</a>.</p>

Thus, our aim is to explore, establish and validate a “Connectomic Deep Brain Stimulation” framework which combines both strands of research to ultimately derive at patient-specific deep brain stimulation therapy that may be informed by individual wiring diagrams of patients that undergo deep brain stimulation surgery.
