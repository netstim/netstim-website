## Hosting and server logs

This is a static informational website. When you visit it, our web server automatically records technical access data in server log files (such as IP address, date and time of the request, requested URL, referrer, and browser user agent). Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR to ensure the technical delivery, stability, and security of the website. Log data is not combined with other data sources and is deleted once it is no longer required for these purposes.

## No cookies or tracking

This website does not use analytics services, advertising trackers, user accounts, or comment forms, and sets no tracking cookies. All fonts are self-hosted. If you toggle the light or dark color theme, your browser stores that preference locally (`localStorage`) on your device only; it is never transmitted to us.

## Embedded media and external links

Recorded talks and audio episodes from third-party platforms (YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, Vimeo with Do Not Track, Spotify, or Libsyn) are not loaded when you open a page. They only load after you actively click to play them; once activated, those providers receive your IP address and may process data under their own privacy policies.

External links (such as journal publisher pages, Google Scholar, or Google Forms for the Stimu-Latte mailing list) lead to external websites whose operators are solely responsible for their data processing.

## Contact by email

If you contact us by email (for example regarding research inquiries or applications), the data you provide is processed solely to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and deleted once it is no longer required, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to request access to your personal data (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), such as the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Düsseldorf.
