<p>The Institute for Network Stimulation is based at University Hospital Cologne.</p>
<p>We use structural, functional and diffusion MRI to study brain networks and how they respond to stimulation. We also develop tools for analyzing imaging and stimulation data in neurological and psychiatric research.</p>
<p><a href="@root@research-interests/">Explore our research</a> or <a href="@root@about-us/about-us/">meet the team</a>.</p>

<h2>Funding</h2>
<p>The Netstim Institute is <strong>currently funded</strong> by the Schilling Foundation and the National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH).</p>
<p><strong>Past funding</strong> includes grants from the German Research Council, the Dystonia Research Foundation, the Berlin Institute of Health, Stiftung Charite, the Thiemann Foundation for Parkinson’s Disease Research, the Foundation for OCD research (FFOR) and the EU Joint Programme&nbsp;– Neurodegenerative Disease Research (JPND).</p>
