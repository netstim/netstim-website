symptoms and anatomy of the GPi”">New SEM study out: “Relating pallidal theta activity to dystonic symptoms and anatomy of the GPi”</a></h2><span class="post-meta"><time datetime="2017-11-13T14:13:00+00:00">November 13, 2017</time>/<a href="@root@new-sem-study-out-relating-pallidal-theta-activity-to-dystonic-symptoms-and-anatomy-of-the-gpi/#respond">0 Comments</a>/in <a href="@root@category/uncategorized/">Uncategorized</a>/by <a href="@root@author/andreashorn/">andreashorn</a></span></header>In a new study (<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/321013451_A_localized_pallidal_physiomarker_in_cervical_dystonia">A localized pallidal physiomarker in cervical dystonia</a>) led by <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Wolf_Julian_Neumann">Wolf-Julian Neumann</a> with <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Siobhan_Ewert">Siobhan Ewert</a>, <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Julius_Huebl">Julius Huebl</a>, <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Christof_Bruecke">Christof Brücke</a>, <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Gerd_Helge_Schneider">Gerd-Helge Schneider</a> and <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Andrea_Kuehn2">Andrea A Kühn</a>, we were able to relate pallidal theta power to dystonic symptom severity for the first time.

Furthermore, using the “Subcortical Electrophysiology Mapping (SEM)” approach developed as part of Lead-DBS (implemented inside the “Lead Group” module), we were able to robustly localize theta activity to an anatomical subpart of the internal pallidum that most likely corresponds to its sensorimotor functional zone (based on an atlas developed by <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Siobhan_Ewert">Siobhan Ewert</a> et al. (<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/317032527_Toward_defining_deep_brain_stimulation_targets_in_MNI_space_A_subcortical_atlas_based_on_multimodal_MRI_histology_and_structural_connectivity">Toward defining deep brain stimulation targets in MNI space:…</a>).

The same approach had been used before to localize beta power in PD within the subthalamic nucleus (<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/315825478_Toward_an_Electrophysiological_Sweet_Spot_for_Deep_Brain_Stimulation_in_the_Subthalamic_Nucleus">Toward an Electrophysiological ” Sweet Spot ” for Deep Brain…</a>) and additionally high frequency oscillations in intraoperative LFP recordings (<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/318676495_Localization_of_beta_and_high-frequency_oscillations_within_the_subthalamic_nucleus_region">Localization of beta and high-frequency oscillations within …</a>).

The approach is described in detail in a blog post (<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org%2F%3Fp%3D2301" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org/?p=2301</a>) and the Lead-DBS knowledge base (<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org%2F%3Fpage_id%3D2403" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=2403</a>) on our website.

We hope that the technique may be applied to localize other LFP signatures to anatomy in the future – again using co-registered recordings of large patient cohorts.

<article aria-label="Lead-DBS 2.0 out now" class="post-item"><div class="archive-media"><a aria-label="two_oh_in_a_nutshell" class="archive-image" href="@root@lead-dbs-2-0-out-now/" title="two_oh_in_a_nutshell"><netstim-image data='{"src":"media/f0f8c0aaa764-two_oh_in_a_nutshell-450x450.png","original":"https://www.netstim.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/two_oh_in_a_nutshell-180x180.png","alt":"","width":180}'/></a></div><div class="standard-content"><header aria-label="Post: Lead-DBS 2.0 out now"><h2><a href="@root@lead-dbs-2-0-out-now/" title="Permanent Link: Lead-DBS 2.0 out now">Lead-DBS 2.0 out now</a></h2><span class="post-meta"><time datetime="2017-09-10T14:17:25+00:00">September 10, 2017</time>/<a href="@root@lead-dbs-2-0-out-now/#respond">0 Comments</a>/in <a href="@root@category/uncategorized/">Uncategorized</a>/by <a href="@root@author/andreashorn/">andreashorn</a></span></header>We are proud to release Lead-DBS 2.0 to the community – an open source tool to localize Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) electrodes in the human brain.

A detailed description of all major new features is available here [<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org%2F%3Fp%3D3174" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org/?p=3174</a>], but in a nutshell they are:

<ul>
<li>DISTAL atlas: A detailed brain atlas of the human subcortex based on histology (graciously provided by <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/M_Chakravarty">M. Mallar Chakravarty</a> and <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Louis_Collins">Louis Collins</a>), MRI and structural connectivity. It is available in three different nomenclatures, thus serving as a rosetta stone for thalamic nomenclatures (See <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Siobhan_Ewert">Siobhan Ewert</a>‘s
<span class="publication-type">Article</span> <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/317032527_Toward_defining_deep_brain_stimulation_targets_in_MNI_space_A_subcortical_atlas_based_on_multimodal_MRI_histology_and_structural_connectivity">Toward defining deep brain stimulation targets in MNI space:…</a>
<p>; for a summary see here [<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org%2F%3Fpage_id%3D2911" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=2911</a>]).</p></li>
<li>FEM-based VTA model (beta): To model the physical effects of DBS in the brain, we teamed up with <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Johannes_Vorwerk">Johannes Vorwerk</a> (SimBio/FieldTrip toolboxes) and Qianqian Fang (Iso2Mesh toolbox). Further help was provided by <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ningfei_Li">Ningfei Li</a> (integration inside Lead-DBS), <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Robert_Oostenveld">Robert Oostenveld</a> (Initial counseling and help with FieldTrip-related code). This is the first open-source pipeline to model volumes of tissue activated (VTA) as a one-step solution (see <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/317381045_Connectivity_Predicts_deep_brain_stimulation_outcome_in_Parkinson_disease">Connectivity Predicts deep brain stimulation outcome in Park…</a>and this page [<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org%2F%3Fpage_id%3D3291" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=3291</a>] for details).</li>
<li>Human Motor Thalamus Atlas: Graciously provided by Igor &amp; Kristy Ilinsky, the human motor thalamus settles controversies about motor projections in the human thalamus. It is based on specialized histological stains and was precisely co-registered to ICBM 2009b space. For more information about the dataset, see [http://<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.humanmotorthalamus.com" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">www.humanmotorthalamus.com</a>].</li>
<li>PaCER: A fully automated method to precisely reconstruct DBS electrode localizations based on postoperative CT data will soon be published by <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Andreas_Husch">Andreas Husch</a> (Hertel group, Luxembourg) and was graciously provided to Lead-DBS. For more information about PaCER, see [<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/https%3A%2F%2Fadhusch.github.io%2FPaCER%2F" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">https://adhusch.github.io/PaCER/</a>].</li>
<li>MER-Mapping: Ari Kappel (Richardson lab, Pittsburgh) and <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Chadwick_Boulay">Chadwick Boulay</a> (Sachs lab, Ottawa) created an intuitive and precise tool to combine DBS reconstructions with microelectrode recordings within Lead-DBS.</li>
<li>Multiple novel atlases: In total, Lead-DBS now comes with 23 subcortical atlases [<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org%2F%3Fpage_id%3D45" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=45</a>] and 14 whole-brain parcellations [<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org%2F%3Fpage_id%3D1004" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=1004</a>] suited for connectomics.</li>
<li>Enhanced workflows: We re-designed large parts of the registration pipeline. For normalization strategies, all SPM routines, ANTs, FSL’s FNIRT and a MaGET-brain like procedure are available directly in Matlab (and without the need to install anything else).</li>
</ul>

We hope you like our new release – and please don’t hesitate to spread the word to colleagues in the DBS field. To download Lead-DBS now, see [<a href="https://www.researchgate.net/deref/http%3A%2F%2Fwww.lead-dbs.org" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">http://www.lead-dbs.org</a>].
