# Site audit, 23 Nov 2026

Scope: every public page of the Hugo build (desktop 1280 px, phone 390 px, light and dark), search, the phone menu, and a comparison with the old WordPress site.

The live www.netstim.org currently returns 404, so the old site was read from the Wayback Machine (June/July 2026 captures). The migration used a newer mirror (Nov 2026), so one or two differences below may be real updates rather than losses.

## 0. Summary

- **Almost nothing was lost.** A sentence-level diff of all 86 old routes against the new build only flags WordPress chrome (comment forms, "Powered by Events Manager", meta strings). Posters still carry affiliations and references inside their dialogs.
- **The problems are structure, stale facts, wordiness and template-looking design.** The migration kept every WordPress page and URL, then put a modern template on top. The result keeps the old site's clutter and adds a generic look.
- **Fix first, because these are wrong rather than ugly:** the Imprint (US address), Andreas Horn's profile (outdated numbers and dead services), the funder logo strip (shows past funders as if current), and empty pages linked from the main menu.

## 3. Structure: what lives where

Today, Research is a catch-all (two research lines, the book, Stimu-Latte's URL, publications' URL). People holds a letter to the reader. Community points to an empty page. Every page has three navigation systems: the dropdown, tabs under the title, and breadcrumbs.

**Proposed map** (Research is research only. The book, Community and Contact each get one clear place.)

```
Home /                      who we are, 2 research lines, latest papers, news, book, team, community, current funders
Research /research/         one overview, two sections:
  Deep Brain Stimulation    ~250 words + figure + link to lead-dbs.org
  Normative Connectomes     ~250 words + figure + link to datasets
Publications /publications/ papers first; tabs: Papers · Posters · Talks; cover art as a strip at the end
People /people/             team with roles, alumni with "now at", jobs as a section (own page only when posts are open)
  /people/<name>/           one profile per person (Andreas: single merged bio)
Book /book/                 top level, featured on Home, in the footer; NOT under Research
News /news/                 one list, year-grouped
Community                   Stimu-Latte /stimu-latte/ · Stimulating Brains (external). Imaging meeting removed.
Footer                      Contact (address, email) · For lab members (manual, links) · Bluesky · Imprint · Privacy
```

- **Book:** a top-level `/book/` page. The old WordPress site already redirected `/the-book/`, so short book links already exist. Feature it on Home and link it in the footer. Fold both book news posts ("available for pre-order" / "available now!") into it and redirect them. The page should state the facts (Academic Press/Elsevier, year, ~600 pages, ISBN 978-0-12-821861-7), have one order button, a 2-sentence blurb, and the **chapter list as a table** (chapter → authors) instead of the current ~900 words of "we were thrilled that X & Y contributed…".
- **About the lab:** the "Dear reader," letter repeats the home hero and the Team intro. Merge About into Home (who we are) and People (funding, contact).
- **Navigation:** pick one secondary system. If Publications and People keep tabs, they don't need dropdowns, and the top bar becomes five plain links: Research · Publications · People · News · Community.
- **URLs:** the current rule is "every URL stays", so the Book sits at `/research-interests/the-book/` and Stimu-Latte at `/research-interests/stimu-latte/`. Clean URLs with 301s from the old ones are cheap with Apache. *Decision for you.*

## 4. Design: what reads as template or "AI slop"

Home:
1. **Black rounded hero card** with a giant tightly tracked headline, a white pill and an "About the lab →" link. This is a generic SaaS-template look. Use a plain hero instead: lab name, one sentence, and the real lab photo or one real figure, full-bleed.
2. **Journal-name wall:** eight journal names in ~44 px type with tiny ↗ arrows, each linking to one arbitrary paper. The heading is a dangling sentence ("Members of our lab published in … such as"). On a phone it becomes eight tall rows. Replace it with "Latest papers", the newest 4–5 entries from `data/publications.yaml`. They update automatically and are much more convincing.
3. **"Two lines of research" shows three cards**, and the third is Lead-DBS, a tool. The Research overview's three cards are DBS, Connectomes and the *book*. Show two lines with two cards, and put Lead-DBS as a link inside the DBS card.
4. **The same sentence appears five times:** "We are a scientific group with interest in neuroimaging, methods development, movement disorders and invasive brain stimulation." It is in the hero (paraphrased), the home team block, About, the Team lead and Andreas's page. Keep it once.
5. **The 3D CSS book** (perspective and shadow) is shown three times: home, the Research card and the Book header. The full jacket image appears again at the bottom of the book page. Use one flat cover, once per page.
6. **Logos floating in big plates:** the small connectome brain on a black tile, the Lead-DBS logo on a black tile, and the Stimu-Latte card as a solid tan block. Either crop to real imagery or drop the image.
7. **The funding block** is a sentence, a "Past funding" link and an 8-logo strip that contradicts the sentence (§1).

All long-form pages (DBS, Connectomes, Book, posts, Andreas, Jobs, Stimu-Latte):
8. **Dead right column.** The text column is ~670 px, left-aligned in a 1,152 px container, so the right ~40% is empty on every article page. The old site had a "Latest updates" sidebar there, which was dropped but its space kept. Either centre a reading column or use the rail for useful facts: book facts and order button, connectome quick links, in-page contents.
9. **Huge H2s on tiny sections.** For example, "Lead-DBS 3.0" is a 44 px heading over two lines of text. Each paragraph is also followed by a stack of "Learn more about X ↗" links that repeat the inline links just above (three in a row at the end of the DBS page). Keep the inline links and drop the duplicate stacks.
10. **"Figures" gallery** on Connectomes: blurry, labels cropped by the 4:3 tiles, generic heading. Show 1–2 figures at full width with captions, or drop it.
11. **"Details" toggles** in the connectome table look like plain text and give no sign that they open.

People:
12. **No roles on team cards** (PI, postdoc, PhD student…) and no "now at" for alumni, which is the first thing visitors look for. Alumni link chips are inconsistent (some people have four, most none).
13. **Pepsi the dog is in 4th place**, between the PI and the rest of the team. Keep Pepsi, but put the dog last.
14. **Grey initials tiles** (FR, MF, CK). Get photos, or use a single neutral silhouette style.

Publications:
15. **19 journal covers come before the paper list**, so the first paper starts ~1,800 px down. Lead with the papers, highlight lab members' names, and move the covers to a strip at the end (or Home).

Smaller items:
16. The breadcrumb shows "STIMU-LATTE" in caps while the title says "Stimu-Latte journal club". The Imaging meeting breadcrumb says "For lab members / Events" while the menu puts it under Community.
17. Search labels the members hub as section "Home". The query "book" returns "For lab members" and "Team".
18. The footer shows lab-internal links (Lab manual, Links, Events) on every public page. Group them under "For lab members", next to Contact.
19. Posts repeat their title as the first line ("Mapping the Dysfunctome"). Almost every excerpt starts "We are excited…". One excerpt shows a raw Mailchimp URL (v1.6.5 post).
20. The book slideshow images all have `alt=""`.
21. Dark mode: the small "Institute for Network Stimulation" text in the logo has low contrast on the dark header.

## 5. Copy: wordy, dated or non-native

- **Lab manual:** 'As is currently "on vogue", we have created a lab-manual … yay!' → "Onboarding, code of conduct and practical information for lab members."
- **Stimu-Latte intro:** 90 words → "A monthly post-doc journal club on neuromodulation. Wednesdays 16:00, [room], Uniklinik Köln. All career levels welcome." Then a sign-up button, the next session, and past sessions as a table (date · presenter · paper).
- **Research intro:** "to ultimately derive at patient-specific…" → "arrive at" (or rewrite).
- **Connectomes:** "data off the Harvard genomic superstruct project" → "data from the Brain Genomics Superstruct Project". "Since 2014, we worked on" → "have worked on". "2016, we replicated" → "In 2016, we replicated".
- **Jobs:** "In second line, we're highly interested…" → "We are also interested in…". "Our research focusses" → "focuses" (and pick British or American spelling once).
- **Posts:** "resources around tis new paper" (typo). Poster "Alzheimer's Diesease" (typo).
- **Funding:** "Stiftung Charite" → "Stiftung Charité". "the German Research Council" appears twice in the old list (the new text dropped the duplicate, fine). It should be "German Research Foundation (DFG)".
- **Book page:** ~1,000 words of who-wrote-which-chapter prose → a chapter table (§3).
- **Andreas page:** three paragraphs that restate the home page, then four more blocks each ending in a button. Cut to a short bio, current role, links.

## 6. Code and maintenance

- **Content is WordPress HTML stored in Markdown/YAML.** `data/people.yaml` has ~500 HTML tags, and the book page is one long line of `<div class="columns"><div class="column column-2-3">…` with `<netstim-image data='{json}'>` markers. Lab members won't be able to edit this. Convert people to structured fields (name, role, photo, links, short bio, status: current/alumni, now_at) and pages to plain Markdown.
- **Over-preservation:** 60 comment feeds, frozen archive pages, Events Manager routes, 69 numeric-ID redirects, and a "copy baseline" check (`tests/copy-baseline.json`) that flags every editorial change as a delta. All of this serves faithfulness to WordPress rather than readers. Once the retire/redirect list is agreed, delete these and keep only a simple old→new redirect map.
- `docs/structure.md`, `design.md` and `editing.md` describe the WordPress-mirroring intent ("retain", "carry over", "original sparse arrangement"). They need rewriting once the direction changes.
