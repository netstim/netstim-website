## Impact of Lead-DBS

<p>In 2015, <a href="http://lead-dbs.org/">Lead-DBS</a> was awarded with the <a href="https://www.google.de/search?q=max+rubner+preis+charite+horn&amp;oq=max+rubn&amp;aqs=chrome.0.69i59j69i57j69i60l4.1653j0j1&amp;sourceid=chrome&amp;ie=UTF-8"><strong>Max-Rubner-Prize</strong> for Innovation</a> by <a href="http://www.stiftung-charite.de/">Stiftung Charité</a>, in 2016 a <strong><a href="https://www.radcliffe.harvard.edu/exploratory-seminars/toward-connectomic-deep-brain-stimulation">Harvard Radcliffe Exploratory Ventures Grant</a></strong> by the <a href="https://www.radcliffe.harvard.edu/">Harvard Radcliffe Institute for Advanced Study</a>. In 2017, Lead-DBS was awarded the <strong>Robert-Koch-Prize</strong> of Charité, Berlin.</p>

<p>At the time of writing, <a href="http://lead-dbs.org/">Lead-DBS</a> was <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=23"><strong>downloaded over 65,000 times</strong></a> by researchers world-wide and currently, <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=21"><strong>groups from over 20 institutions contributed</strong></a> to <a href="http://lead-dbs.org/">Lead-DBS</a> code &amp; content.</p>

## Lead-DBS 3.0

<p>In 2017, <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053811918307663">Lead-DBS 2.0</a> was introduced which featured five major <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053811918307663">contributions from various authors</a> across the globe. In 2023, <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053811923000113">Lead-DBS 3.0</a> was introduced, which was the largest update to our toolbox yet.</p>

## Lead-DBS methods

<p>Since 2012, we introduced a number of novel methods to analyze DBS recording sites and brain connectivity. As such, the <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=2403">subcortical electrophysiology mapping</a> (SEM) approach was introduced in <a href="http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/hbm.23594/abstract">Horn et al. 2017a</a> and applied in <a href="http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ana.25095/abstract">Neumann et al. 2017</a>. A summary of the approach can <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=2403">be found here</a>. A novel method to convert AC/PC coordinates into MNI standard space was introduced in <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/28163141">Horn et al. 2017b</a> and is <a href="http://www.lead-dbs.org/?page_id=2500">described here</a>. Finally, a method to predict clinical outcome of DBS patients based on brain connectivity – termed Connectivity Benefit Mapping – was introduced in <a href="http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ana.24974/abstract">Horn et al. 2017c</a>.</p>
